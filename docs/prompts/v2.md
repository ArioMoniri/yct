# TRIBE v2 × YC Application Videos — Master Prompt v2

v2 replaces v1 (`docs/prompts/v1_original.md`). It folds Prompt A (curation) and Prompt B (normalisation and TRIBE inference) into one master prompt. Every change traces to a council review in `reviews/prompt_v1/`; the tags [W#] watchdog, [S#] strict referee, [B#] biostatistician and [N#] neuroscientist name the source. Where reviewers conflicted, the PI's resolution is in `DECISIONS.md` (D-001 to D-012). Verified facts about the model live in `docs/tribe_requirements.md`. A fact not in that file is UNKNOWN until the Phase 0 smoke test confirms it.

## 0. QUESTION, SCOPE AND HONEST FRAMING

**Question.** Among founder-posted, public YC application videos with publicly reported outcomes, do *TRIBE v2-predicted average-subject cortical responses* separate accepted from rejected videos? Do they add out-of-sample predictive value beyond (a) hand-built stimulus features and (b) generic pretrained encoder features?

**What this can and cannot show** [S1, S4, N-claims]
- TRIBE v2 is a deterministic function of the stimulus. Any "beyond stimulus" result means *beyond the stimulus features we measured*. It does not mean the brain carries extra information.
  - A positive H4 may be described only as "TRIBE's representation is a useful inductive bias / dimensionality reduction for this task".
  - A claim that the effect is brain-specific additionally requires M1 ≥ M0b at equal dimensionality (§1).
- The population is founder-posted public videos with public outcomes. This is not all YC applicants. Survivorship and self-selection are expected, especially among rejected videos.
- YC decisions depend mostly on non-video information, so the realistic effect is small (AUC roughly 0.52–0.60). The study is powered only for large effects. At 30 per group the minimum detectable effect (MDE) is d ≈ 0.74 / AUC ≈ 0.70 [B-power].
- **Required wording.** Use "TRIBE v2-predicted (average-subject) cortical response". Never write "brain activity", "engagement", "persuasion", "trust" or "the brain prefers".
- **Units.** Predictions are in relative units. TRIBE's training targets were z-scored per session [N-fact 3]. Never report or interpret absolute or |BOLD| amplitude. The pilot's "+12% mean |BOLD|" is not interpretable, and no hypothesis is built on it.
- **Licence.** TRIBE v2 is CC-BY-NC-4.0. This is non-commercial research only. No pitch-scoring product or service may come out of it [W6, N-fact 11].

**Pilot** (1 accepted vs 1 rejected; for context only)
- The two pilot videos are excluded from every analysis, identified by sha256 [S3].
- H2's regions are defined from an atlas, not from the pilot maps. H2 is labelled "directional hypothesis motivated by a 1 vs 1 inspection".

## 1. HYPOTHESES AND ANALYSIS FAMILY (locked at Phase 6)

**Primary features (resolution D-003)** [B1, B-SAP, N-window]
- Compute the seven Yeo-7 network means of the *spatially standardised* prediction, time-averaged over the common analysis window W.
  - Spatially standardised means: at each TR, centre and scale across non-medial-wall vertices.
  - Do NOT z-score per vertex over time for mean features. It makes every mean exactly 0 [B1].
- Window W is set in Phase 4, before unblinding, from the label-free group-mean time course. The default is TRs [5, 45) of the trimmed video, i.e. 40 TRs at a TR of 1.0 s.
  - Do NOT apply an extra HRF shift. TRIBE outputs are already shifted 5 s [N-fact 2].
  - The first TRs are dropped because of the encoders' context warm-up, not because of HRF lag.

**Comparator models** [S1, B-SAP]
- **M0 (stimulus features):** hand-built stimulus features (§5, Phase 3).
- **M0b (generic encoders):** the same frozen backbones TRIBE uses (V-JEPA2, w2v-BERT, Llama-3.2-3B), time-averaged over W, then PCA-reduced inside each CV fold to k = 7 (the same k as M1).
- **M1 (TRIBE):** the 7 TRIBE network features.
- **M2:** M0 + M1.

**Hypotheses**

| ID | Estimand | Test | Correction | α |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **H4 (sole primary)** | ΔAUC = AUC(M2) − AUC(M0). AUC is the mean pooled out-of-fold AUC over 20× repeated StratifiedGroupKFold(5), groups = company, using L2-logistic regression with nested C | Conditional permutation test: brain features are residualised on M0 inside each fold, then permuted within exchangeability blocks; B = 2,000; one-sided | none | .05 |
| H1 | Any Yeo-7 network differs | maxT over 7 Welch t-tests (Westfall–Young), B = 10,000, two-sided | Holm across H1–H3 + H4b | .05 FWER |
| H2 | Accepted > rejected on the language/social composite (atlas-defined pSTS, TP, L IFG, TPJ parcels, listed in the prereg) | Welch t, permutation B = 10,000, one-sided | Holm (same family) | .05 |
| H3 | AUC(M1) > .5 | Full nested-pipeline label permutation, B = 2,000 | Holm (same family) | .05 |
| H4b | AUC(M1) − AUC(M0b) at equal k | Paired permutation of out-of-fold predictions within blocks, B = 2,000, two-sided | Holm (same family) | .05 |

**Exchangeability blocks** [B5]
- Companies with one video: permute labels within prespecified era strata (≤2016, 2017–2020, ≥2021; finalised in Phase 1 before any labels are seen by analysts).
- Companies with two videos: swap the labels within the pair.
- Every p-value is computed as (b+1)/(B+1).

**Effect sizes**
- Hedges g with a company-cluster BCa bootstrap CI (10,000 resamples).
- ΔAUC with a Nadeau–Bengio-corrected CI. This interval is descriptive only.
- Also report AUC, balanced accuracy and Brier score. Never report PPV or any implied real-world hit rate [B10].

**Two estimands** [B5]
- Unadjusted, and adjusted. The adjusted estimate uses Freedman–Lane under the same blocks. The nuisance set is the first min(5, ⌊N/15⌋) PCs of M0.
- Global signal is NOT a covariate in the primary analysis, because it is a probable mediator.

**SESOI and nulls** [B9]
- SESOI: |d| = 0.5 per network; ΔAUC = 0.05.
- Run TOST at ±0.5 for H1/H2. A result that is non-significant and not equivalent is labelled **inconclusive**. It may never be described as "no difference".

**Everything else is EXPLORATORY**
- This covers parcels (BH-FDR within feature type), vertex TFCE (H = 2, E = 1, mesh adjacency), T2 progress-bin cluster-mass, spin-test enrichment of the effect map, modality ablations, SVM, leave-one-year-out, within-company pairs (sign-flip; descriptive if fewer than 10 pairs), and the mixed model (only if at least 15 companies have multiple videos).
- Exploratory results are reported with estimates and CIs, and labelled as exploratory.

**Holdout** [B8]
- Use a locked holdout only if the confidence-A total is at least 200 with at least 80 per class.
- Otherwise there is no holdout. Videos found after the prereg lock form a temporal replication set, analysed once.

## 2. COUNCIL (slimmed; decision D-001)

The executing agent is the PI/Orchestrator. It runs the engineering roles (Curator, Preprocessing, Modeling, Reporter) itself. These roles do the work and **do not vote** [S-delete, W1].

**Voting reviewers.** Each reviewer is a separate subagent with fresh context.

| Reviewer | Owns | Power |
|---|---|---|
| Watchdog | integrity, provenance, cost, secrets, legal, blinding (§2b) | VETO on any gate |
| Strict Referee (also the Skeptic) | construct validity, confounds, leakage, overclaiming | STOP |
| Biostatistician | SAP, power, CV, permutation design | STOP on inference validity |
| Neuroscientist | TRIBE usage, atlases, ROIs, controls, functional claims | STOP on functional claims |
| QA | tests, CI, reproducibility (`make` rebuild) | merge block on red |

**Anti-theatre rules** [W1]
1. Reviewers receive only the repository at a commit SHA plus the gate packet's *artifact list*. They never receive the author's narrative, conclusions or other reviews.
2. Every review must do one of two things:
   - cite at least 3 concrete defects, each with file:line or run-id plus metric; or
   - write "searched X, Y, Z; found none", listing what was searched.
3. A vote without evidence is void.
4. Reviews whose wording overlaps by more than 40% (shared 8-grams) are invalid and must be redone.
5. Each review logs its subagent id.
6. The red-team reviewer rotates every gate. The Strict Referee and Biostatistician alternate as "assume this is wrong; find why".

**Decision rule**
- GO requires: no STOP, no Watchdog VETO, and at least 4/5 GO.
- Any FIX opens a ticket in BUGS.md or ENHANCEMENTS.md. The phase is then re-gated, and the re-gate uses fresh reviewers.
- Maximum 3 FIX cycles per gate. After that, write `reviews/escalations/<gate>.md` and stop for the human.
- The PI logs every decision in DECISIONS.md, which is append-only and checked by CI.

**Human-only actions** [W9]
- Signing `DATA_DECISION.md`.
- Issuing the `UNBLIND_TOKEN`.
- Raising the budget.
- Lifting a Watchdog veto.
- Changing the prereg after the lock.
- Making anything public.
- Prolific/IRB.
- Rotating credentials.

## 2b. WATCHDOG AND INTEGRITY RULES (from reviews/prompt_v1/watchdog.md §2b; binding)

**2b.1 Independence**
- The Watchdog is a fresh subagent on every invocation. It holds no other role, writes no project code, and never sees the sealed labels.
- Its inputs are the repository at a SHA, `outputs/runs/`, the GPU ledger and the Lambda instance list. It never sees author narratives.

**2b.2 Powers**
- It may VETO a gate, terminate any GPU instance, freeze a work stream, require a re-derivation by a fresh agent, and open incidents.
- It may not change code, data, tests or the prereg, and it may not unblind.
- Only the human can lift a veto, and only by writing it in DECISIONS.md.

**2b.3 Cadence.** The Watchdog runs at three points:
- after every gate packet, before the votes are counted;
- in at least one random spot audit per working day, timed by `random.Random(date_seed)`;
- on any trigger: budget at 80%, a heartbeat older than 10 minutes, CI red, a protected path touched, a STOP vote, or a stuck escalation.

**2b.4 Checklist.** Each check gets the command it ran and its result.
1. **Provenance.** Every metric or test claim links to `outputs/runs/<run_id>/` with the SHA and exit code. Re-run 1 random test file and 1 random metric.
2. **Labels.** `verify_labels.py` passes. Every evidence hash resolves to a stored snapshot. Re-derive at least 10 random label rows blind.
3. **Goalposts.** The prereg hash is unchanged since the `prereg-lock` tag. Logs are append-only. No test assertion has been loosened (diff check). Frozen config keys are unchanged.
4. **Blinding.** Grep for `outcome|accepted|rejected` outside `data/sealed/` and `src/yct/curate/`. Confirm there is no UNBLIND_TOKEN before Phase 6 GO.
5. **Council.** Every required review exists, has a subagent id, cites evidence, and passes the overlap check.
6. **Cost.** Every Lambda instance is in the ledger, its heartbeat is under 10 minutes, and it is within its lifetime and the budget. Terminate orphans now.
7. **Secrets.** gitleaks is clean on the full history, and the logs contain no key-like strings.
8. **Data/legal.** `DATA_DECISION.md` is signed before any download. Retention is honoured. No founder names or faces appear in outputs. The NC licence is respected.
9. **Loops.** Nothing is past its stuck bound without an escalation file.
10. **Fabrication.** Pick 3 random factual claims (a TRIBE spec, a URL, a citation) and verify each against its source.

**2b.5 Outputs**
- Each audit writes `reviews/watchdog/<YYYYMMDD>_<gate|spot>_<n>.md` with a verdict of PASS, VETO or INCIDENT.
- Each audit appends a line to `reviews/watchdog/audit_log.jsonl`, which is append-only and checked by CI.
- A CRITICAL incident (leak, orphan GPU, unblinding, fabrication) means stopping and notifying the human.

**2b.6 Integrity rules for every agent**
- No claim without an artifact. "I checked" with no run-id counts as unchecked.
- Never invent a URL, video, label, quote, citation, model parameter or test result. Write UNKNOWN and open a ticket instead.
- Never edit tests, thresholds, the prereg or logs to make something pass.
- Never read `data/sealed/`.
- Never leave a GPU running without a heartbeat. When in doubt, terminate it.
- Report your own mistakes immediately in BUGS.md. Concealment is the only unrecoverable error.

**2b.7 Fallibility**
- Every 3rd gate, a second Watchdog audits the same SHA.
- Disagreements between the two go to the human.

## 3. ENGINEERING, BLINDING, COMPUTE

**TDD and configuration**
- TDD: write the failing pytest first. Never weaken a test.
- To change a test, open a ticket, get the QA vote and get Watchdog sign-off.
- Every tunable lives in `config.yaml`. Keys marked `frozen: true` cannot change after their phase gate (CI-checked).

**Provenance**
- Each `make` target writes `outputs/runs/<run_id>/` containing: the command, the git SHA, the config hash, input hashes, the exit code, logs and metrics.
- No result exists unless it has a run directory.

**Pins** [N-fact 10]
- Python ≥ 3.11; torch < 2.7; exca == 0.5.20.
- The TRIBE repo commit and the HF revision are recorded, along with the `best.ckpt` sha256 (`docs/tribe_requirements.md`).
- WhisperX is pinned in our own environment; never use the unpinned `uvx` path (see Phase 2).
- Everything else is pinned in `requirements.lock`.

**Blinding** [W2]
- The Curator writes outcome labels only to `data/sealed/labels.csv.age`, encrypted with `age` to the human owner's public key `docs/owner_age.pub`.
- `data/manifest.csv` has NO outcome column.
- Until the human commits `UNBLIND_TOKEN`, analysis code reads `data/labels_permuted.csv`: labels permuted with a seed that is unknown to analysts and stored in the sealed file.
- The Curator subagent never sees the hypotheses section of this prompt [W9].
- The Phase 3 confound × outcome table is produced only after unblinding [S-blinding-leak].

**Compute and cost** [W5]
- **EC2 pod** (`infra/pod.env`, eu-central-1, CPU, accessed via SSM): orchestration, curation, ffmpeg and stats.
- **GPU work**: rented on Lambda Cloud through `infra/lambda_gpu.py` only. This covers TRIBE inference, the backbones for M0b, and WhisperX.
  - Instance: `gpu_1x_a100*` (40–80 GB). The A10 (24 GB) is allowed only after the P0 smoke test measures peak VRAM below 20 GB [N-fact 6].
  - Budget cap: `BUDGET_USD`, default 50 until the human sets it. An alert fires at 80%.
  - Each instance has a mandatory `--max-hours` of at most 4.
  - Each instance sends a heartbeat every 2 minutes. It is killed after 10 minutes of silence, or after 15 minutes below 5% GPU use.
  - `reap` runs at session start, at every gate and after every job.
  - Never run TRIBE on the EC2 pod.

**Secrets** [W7]
- Secrets come only from the environment (`LAMBDA_API_KEY`, `HF_TOKEN`, AWS).
- Never print or commit them, and redact them in logs.
- gitleaks runs in pre-commit and in CI.
- If a secret leaks: revoke it, rotate it, record an incident, and notify the human.

**Stuck rule** [W8]
- A task counts as stuck if any of these happens:
  - 3 failed fix attempts;
  - 2 hours on one item;
  - the same error 3 times;
  - 90 minutes with no commit.
- When stuck, write `reviews/escalations/` and move to other unblocked work, or stop.
- At most 5 enhancement tickets per phase. Enhancements are frozen after the prereg lock, except EXPLORATORY ones.

**Debug and enhance loop.** Unchanged from v1 §4:
1. Log the problem.
2. Reproduce it minimally and write a failing regression test.
3. Find the root cause: state a hypothesis, gather evidence, record what was ruled out.
4. Fix it and run the full suite.
5. Rerun stale downstream steps, chosen by hash.
6. Record before/after metrics and re-gate.

## 5. ROADMAP

Detailed tasks, tests and DoD are in `ROADMAP.md`. Phases in brief, with the v2 changes marked ★:

- **P0 Setup.**
  - CI with gitleaks, the prereg/append-only guards and the frozen-key check ★.
  - TRIBE smoke test on a 10 s clip on a Lambda A100. It records peak VRAM, runtime, the output shape (T, 20484) and the TR ★.
  - An **impulse clip** to measure the actual latency of the output response ★.
  - A determinism rerun with an empty feature cache ★.
- **P1 Curation.**
  - Search, label and exclude as in Prompt A v1, with these additions:
    - an evidence snapshot (page HTML/PDF, sha256) per label ★;
    - `verify_labels.py` checks each quote verbatim and recomputes the A-rule ★;
    - a fresh agent re-derives a 15% sample of labels, and the human spot-checks 10 rows ★;
    - pilot videos are excluded ★;
    - labels are sealed ★.
  - Record the era strata and the within-company pairs.
  - **Stop rules:** fewer than 20 confidence-A videos per group means a descriptive pilot only. 20–29 per group is confirmatory but flagged as underpowered, with the MDE reported ★.
- **P2 Normalisation** (TRIBE-aware) ★ [N-crit].
  - Keep the native aspect ratio. Scale the short side to 256 (or more), with NO letterbox or pillarbox padding: V-JEPA2 centre-crops to a square, so padding would enter the crop.
  - Record the crop coverage (the fraction of the frame and of face area outside the central square) as a confound.
  - Use a CFR of 30 fps (the backbone samples at 2 Hz).
  - Trim black frames and silence.
  - Audio is 16 kHz mono. Loudness normalisation goes to −23 LUFS (speech-gated) for hygiene only; w2v-BERT z-scores each chunk anyway. The mono phase check stays.
  - Quality floor: a 480p round-trip copy.
  - 9:16 videos form their own stratum.
- **P2b Transcripts.**
  - Run pinned WhisperX large-v3 on the GPU and write `<stem>.tsv` in TRIBE's event format. TRIBE then reuses it instead of calling `uvx` [N-fact 4].
  - Purge stale `.wav`/`.tsv` files and the feature cache before every run (a test enforces this).
  - Report WER on 5 hand-checked transcripts, and an accent flag.
- **P3 Stimulus features.**
  - M0 features as in v1, plus crop coverage, caption flag and era.
  - Also extract the M0b backbone embeddings.
  - Only label-free distributions are produced before unblinding ★.
- **P4 TRIBE inference.**
  - Every video: one model, one seed, empty cache, a fixed batch size, fp settings as shipped.
  - Ablations via `config_update={"data.features_to_use": [...]}`; zero-fill is in-distribution [N-fact 7]. The text path includes TTS, so it is labelled "text+TTS".
  - QC: finite values, shape, T, outliers, and determinism on 3 videos (≤1e-4, or documented).
  - Set W from the label-free mean time course.
- **P4b Controls gate** ★ (STOP if this fails) [S5, N-controls].
  - **Positive controls**, within videos:
    - face-present vs face-absent TRs → fusiform/FFC (HCP-MMP) d > 0.8;
    - speech vs silence TRs → STG/A4–A5 d > 0.8;
    - V1–V3 tracks motion and cuts.
  - **OOD check**: predicted-variance distributions vs ≥5 reference naturalistic clips.
  - **Reliability mask**: run TRIBE on held-out naturalistic movie data where we have measured fMRI, if obtainable. Otherwise, a standard signal-dropout mask, documented [N-fact 8].
  - **Negative-control labels**, analysed blind with the full pipeline: upload-date parity (expected null) and aspect ratio (a known stimulus confound, expected detectable).
- **P5 Features (blinded).**
  - Atlases: Schaefer-400/7 from the CBIG fsaverage5 `.annot`; Yeo-7 derived from the Schaefer names; HCP-MMP for ROIs; Destrieux only for labelling.
  - Mask the medial wall.
  - fsaverage5 = the first 10,242 vertices per hemisphere; no nearest-vertex mapping is needed ★.
  - Compute spatially standardised features (primary) and global amplitude G (secondary).
  - Test: the pipeline is reproducible from cached predictions.
- **P6 Prereg lock.**
  - Commit `docs/preregistration.md` = §1 + the SAP, with W, N, MDE, strata and the ROI parcel lists filled in.
  - Tag it `prereg-lock`.
  - Unanimous 5/5 GO and a Watchdog PASS.
  - **The human** issues the UNBLIND_TOKEN.
  - Collection is closed.
- **P7 Group statistics** (H1, H2, adjusted estimands, exploratory analyses).
  - Validation, run before unblinding: null FPR within 3.7–6.4% over 1,000 shuffles, and recovery of planted effects at d = 0.5 and 0.8.
- **P8 Prediction** (H4 primary, H3, H4b).
  - Leakage test: no company overlap across folds.
  - Shuffled-label AUC ≈ 0.5.
  - Determinism.
- **P9 Interpretation.**
  - Networks and HCP-MMP labels, always tied to the M0 covariate and ablation results.
  - No Neurosynth reverse inference ★.
  - All map figures show lateral, medial, ventral and dorsal views.
  - The subcortical gap is stated as a limitation.
- **P10 Report.**
  - Every number is generated from results files.
  - The build fails if any referenced result is missing.
  - H1–H4/H4b are each stated as supported, not supported, or inconclusive (with power).
  - The pilot claims are listed and each marked confirmed or not.
  - Deviations from the prereg are listed.

**Deferred** (not in v2 scope; requires human-run IRB) [S-delete]
- Phase 9b, the Prolific rater study.
- A behavioural-validation addendum, if this study finds anything.

**Deleted as disproportionate to N** [S-delete]
- The 20% holdout at small N (see §1).
- A content-segmentation alignment pass drafted by an LLM. N6 is kept exploratory only, with human κ, if time allows.
- The lateralisation index as a test (descriptive only).

## 7. STOP CONDITIONS (write an escalation and wait for the human)

- **Data.**
  - Fewer than 20 confidence-A videos per group means a descriptive pilot only.
  - `DATA_DECISION.md` is unsigned.
- **Model.**
  - TRIBE cannot run, or its outputs are non-deterministic beyond tolerance with an empty cache.
  - The P4b positive controls fail.
- **Confounds.**
  - The confounds alone give M0 AUC ≥ 0.85; H4 then becomes exploratory, and we continue only if the human agrees.
  - An era-outcome confound that the strata cannot address.
- **Process.**
  - More than 3 FIX cycles at one gate.
  - Any Watchdog CRITICAL incident.
  - Budget at 100%.

Start at Phase 0. After every gate, post a short status: phase, decision, key metrics with run-ids, next step.
