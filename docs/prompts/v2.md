## 0. QUESTION, SCOPE AND HONEST FRAMING

**Question.** Among founder-posted, public YC application videos with publicly reported outcomes, do *TRIBE v2-predicted average-subject cortical responses* separate accepted from rejected videos? Do they add out-of-sample predictive value beyond (a) hand-built stimulus features and (b) generic pretrained encoder features?

**What this can and cannot show** [S1, S4, N-claims]
- TRIBE v2 is a fixed function of the stimulus (reproducibility policy: §5 P4, D-038). "Beyond M0" means beyond the span of the prereg-frozen Q(M0) listed in §1 H4 (v2.5 C3; D-037): every linear M0 term (after any pre-registered merges), the forced upload-metadata covariate `platform_nonYT` (v2.7 FR), the era dummies, speech_rate², speech_fraction², speech_rate·speech_fraction, and exactly the further quadratic terms the rule admits at the final N. It is **not** beyond all quadratic functions of the measured features: quadratic terms outside the listed span are not controlled. The prereg prints the Q column list, the merges applied and the excluded terms; P10 repeats this sentence beside every H4 result. It does not mean the brain carries extra information, and it is not evidence of brain-specificity.
  - A positive H4 may be described only as "TRIBE's representation carries outcome-predictive information not captured by the listed span of our measured stimulus features and upload metadata (Q(M0), §1 H4)" (v2.7 FR: M0 holds the label-free `platform_nonYT` metadata term; `upload_lag_days`, possibly outcome-caused, is sensitivity-only).
  - H4b (vs generic encoders) is a descriptive estimate. No design here licenses a "brain-specific" claim (v2.1 S6).
- The population is founder-posted public videos with public outcomes. This is not all YC applicants. Survivorship and self-selection are expected, especially among rejected videos.
- YC decisions depend mostly on non-video information, so the realistic effect is small (AUC roughly 0.52–0.60). The study is powered only for large effects. At 30 per group [B-power; v2.1 S3]:
  - a single network t-test has an MDE of d ≈ 0.74, i.e. AUC ≈ 0.70;
  - **corrected (v2.6 P1; D-046, Biostat P1 #1):** the **primary H4** at d ≈ 1.06 of signal beyond M0 reaches ~80% power only at N ≈ 100 (≈ 50 per group) in the committed `analysis/power/h4sim.py` (an optimistic simulation). Reproduced power at eff 1.06: **0.50 at N = 56**, 0.46 at N = 60, 0.72 at N = 80, 0.79 at N = 100 (eff 1.5 at N = 56: 0.68). The earlier "≈ 80% at 30 per group" did not reproduce (it came from an uncommitted scratch variant). The rerun with ≥ 500 datasets and a pinned seed is committed before the prereg lock and these values are replaced by it.
  - ~~**Wave 1** (56 companies, one per company; worst case 30/26) is exploratory: single-network MDE d ≈ 0.76 (Holm worst case ≈ 0.92–0.95), H4 MDE > 1.5.~~ **Restated (v2.7 FR; D-053, Biostat/Referee P1 c2 B2):** the wave-1 **analysis set** is `in_primary & qc_pass` = **53 videos from 53 companies** (D-050; E12 10 / E3 43; non-YouTube 7). It is **exploratory**. No outcome split is used here: the MDEs are label-free bounds from `analysis/power/pw.py 53` (two-sided α = .05, power .80) over the balanced split 27/26 and the worst split the ≥ 20-per-group stop rule allows, 33/20: single-network d = **0.785–0.809** (AUC 0.710–0.716); Holm worst case (α/4, family H1–H3 + H4b) d = **0.947–0.976**; Bonferroni over the 7 networks d = **1.006–1.038**. H4 at N = 53 (`analysis/power/h4sim.py lin <eff> resid 500 53`, seeds 1000+i, B = 99, 2 CV repeats): power **H4POW106** at eff 1.06 and **H4POW15** at eff 1.5, i.e. H4 MDE > 1.5. Wave-1 nulls cannot rule out realistic effects (AUC 0.52–0.60). The owner-side group totals are not restated here (they stay sealed until the wave-2 trigger, D-046).
- In practice H4 can detect only very large incremental effects. Prereg and report must state this.
- **Required wording.** Use "TRIBE v2-predicted (average-subject) cortical response". Never write "brain activity", "engagement", "persuasion", "trust" or "the brain prefers".
- **Timing** (v2.5 C3; D-039). TRIBE predictions are temporally smeared: P0 shows a response plateau starting before the event (bidirectional context plus the 5 s output offset; ±2 TR uncertainty). Latency is stated as "L ≤ 0 TR (±2 TR plateau)". No within-video timing or onset claim finer than 5 s is made, and the T2 progress bins are never read as event-locked.
- **Units.** Predictions are in relative units. TRIBE's training targets were z-scored per session (tribe_requirements.md). Never report or interpret absolute or |BOLD| amplitude. The pilot's "+12% mean |BOLD|" is not interpretable, and no hypothesis is built on it.
- **Licence.** TRIBE v2 is CC-BY-NC-4.0. This is non-commercial research only. No pitch-scoring product or service may come out of it [W6, tribe_requirements.md].

**Pilot** (1 accepted vs 1 rejected; for context only)
- The two pilot videos are excluded from every analysis, identified by sha256 [S3].
- H2's regions are defined from an atlas, not from the pilot maps. H2 is labelled "directional hypothesis motivated by a 1 vs 1 inspection".

## 1. HYPOTHESES AND ANALYSIS FAMILY (locked at Phase 6)

**Primary features, M1 (k = 7)** (D-003, revised in v2.1 S4) [B1, B-SAP]
- Compute the *spatially standardised* prediction: at each TR, centre and scale across non-medial-wall vertices.
  - The medial wall is ONE mask used everywhere (M1 standardisation, network and parcel means, H2 ROIs, peak tables, maps): the union of the Schaefer-400 medial wall (label 0), HCP-MMP1 `???` and Destrieux `Medial_wall` = 993 LH + 987 RH = 1,980 fsaverage5 vertices. Destrieux `Medial_wall` is never reported as an anatomical label. A NaN on any cortical vertex is an error, never silently propagated (v2.4 G-D032; D-034).
- Average it over the window W and take network means.
  - The 7 network means of a spatially centred map sum to about 0, so they have rank 6.
  - Use the 6 non-limbic Yeo networks as M1 features. Limbic is the omitted reference: its mean is the exact linear combination −Σ(n_k/n_L)m_k, so no information is lost. Limbic (OFC/temporal pole) is also susceptible to signal dropout in the fMRI TRIBE was trained on, so it is not interpreted. No valuation/reward claim is made (v2.2 N-M3).
  - H1 nevertheless tests all 7 Yeo networks (the limbic test is redundant but legitimate); M1 uses 6 (v2.2 B-minor).
- Add as the 7th feature the time-averaged **spatial SD** of the raw prediction. This keeps the spatial-contrast signal that standardisation removes.
- Do NOT z-score per vertex over time for mean features. It makes every mean exactly 0 [B1].
- Global amplitude G is a secondary feature. Its level is relative, because TRIBE targets were z-scored (tribe_requirements §3).

**T1 input and window W** (v2.1 S7)
- T1 feeds TRIBE a clip trimmed to its **first 58.0 s** (ffprobe duration ≤ 58.5 s asserted). A test asserts that the events dataframe holds exactly one Audio and one Video chunk per clip (tribe_requirements §2.2, §3) (v2.2 N-M1).
- Default W = [max(5, 4 + L + 1), that + 40) TRs, where L is the P0 impulse latency rounded up to whole TRs (L = 0 gives [5, 45); P0 measured L ≤ 0 TR, ±2 TR plateau). Videos whose trimmed duration is < W_end + 5 s (`window.min_tail_s` = 5; was 2 s; the end-of-clip rise spans the last ~4 TRs) are excluded from T1 (logged) and kept in T2 (v2.2 N-M2; v2.5 C3, D-039).
- W is set once in P4 from the label-free group-mean time course and the P0 impulse-clip latency. It is then frozen *before* any M0/M0b window-averaged feature is computed.
- Do NOT add an extra HRF shift: TRIBE outputs are already offset by 5 s (README l.32). Measured latency enters only through L in W above (v2.2 N-M2). The first TRs are dropped because of the encoders' context warm-up.

**Comparator models** [S1, B-SAP]
- **M0 (stimulus features):** hand-built stimulus features (§5, Phase 3).
  - The M0 feature list and p are frozen in the prereg. Binary and categorical features are not squared (v2.2 B-D5).
  - Assembly (`src/yct/features/m0.py`, config `m0:`) (v2.4 G-D032):
    - Per-TR confounds are averaged over the frozen W (W-averaging step), per video.
    - Visual features are computed inside the V-JEPA2 view (short edge → 292, centre 256²): `luminance_crop`, `contrast_crop`, `motion_energy_crop`, `face_area_crop_frac`.
    - `text_frac` is NOT in M0 (on-screen text detection is not implemented); it is never imputed.
    - Words/s (`speech_rate_wps`) and speech fraction over W are in M0; they, their squares and their product are always in Q(M0) (TRIBE known issue #8; D-037).
    - Label-free imputation, pre-specified: no face in W → face area 0; no voiced frame in W → pitch = median over the videos with voiced speech, plus a binary `voiced_any` indicator (linear only, never squared or in products); a missing transcript → hard failure; any other non-finite value → hard failure. M0 is asserted finite.
    - **Q(M0) rule (v2.5 C3; D-037; `m0.q_m0`, config `m0:`).** No PCA. All columns are computed on the full analysis set after exclusions, label-free; z = (x − mean)/SD with ddof 0. In order:
      1. [1, z(speech_rate_wps), z(speech_frac), z(other continuous M0), `voiced_any`, `platform_nonYT`, era dummies]: **all** linear M0 terms, always (`platform_nonYT` = forced upload-metadata covariate, binary, linear only; v2.7 FR, D-053).
      2. speech_rate², speech_fraction², speech_rate·speech_fraction: always.
      3. Optional quadratic terms in a fixed prereg order: squares of the other continuous features by descending label-free variance of z², then all remaining pairwise products by descending variance of z_a·z_b (ties by name). Each term is added only while it raises the numerical rank, until rank = ⌊N/3⌋.
      - Numerical rank = number of singular values > 10⁻⁸·σ_max (SVD; not the column count). Aliased columns are dropped and logged (they do not change the span); cond(Q) ≤ 10⁸ is asserted.
      - If blocks 1 + 2 alone exceed ⌊N/3⌋, the pre-registered, label-free **merge list** (`m0.merge_list`) is applied in order, one step at a time and only as far as needed, before any optional term: (i) luminance_crop + contrast_crop → photometric composite; (ii) motion_energy_crop + scene_cuts → visual-change composite; (iii) rms_db + pitch_mean_hz → voice-acoustics composite. A composite is the mean of its members' z-scores. Speech rate, speech fraction and face area are never merged. If the list is exhausted, Q fails hard; only a DECISIONS entry before P6 can change M0.
      - ~~Planned dimensions (9 continuous M0 + `voiced_any` + 3 eras …): N = 40 → 13, 3 merges, 0 optional; N = 60 → 20, 4 squares; N = 80 → 26, 7 squares + 3 products.~~ **Planned dimensions (v2.7 FR; D-053; G-D032 c3 Biostat #2, Referee N1):** 9 continuous M0 + `voiced_any` + `platform_nonYT` + **2 era blocks** (E12/E3: 1 dummy) + 3 forced = **16 required columns**, generic data; `m0.q_dimension_table` reads the era blocks and covariates from config and is frozen in `tests/test_m0_covariates.py`:

        | N | ⌊N/3⌋ | merges | optional terms | rank |
        |---|---|---|---|---|
        | 46 | 15 | 1 (photometric_crop) | 0 | 15 |
        | 50 | 16 | 0 | 0 | 16 |
        | **53** | **17** | **0** | **1** | **17** |
        | 56 | 18 | 0 | 2 | 18 |

        S-LAG sensitivity (+ `upload_lag_days`, `lag_unknown` = 18 required): N = 46 → 3 merges; 50 → 2; **53 → 1 merge (photometric_crop), 0 optional**; 56 → 0 merges, 0 optional. In the YouTube-only sensitivity `platform_nonYT` is constant, so it is aliased and frees one column (N = 46: no merge). A constant `voiced_any` is aliased likewise. At N = 53 Q is saturated except for one optional term, so almost every quadratic term is outside the span: P5b's diffuse g is expected to FAIL "beyond M0" (Biostat c3 N2). The prereg reprints the table at the final N.
  - **Label-free exclusions** (logged with reason): no audio track, or fewer than 20 words in W (known issue #7). They apply to every analysis (v2.4 G-D032).
- **M0b (generic encoders):** the same frozen backbones TRIBE uses (V-JEPA2, w2v-BERT, Llama-3.2-3B), time-averaged over W.
  - Each encoder block is z-scored, then PCA-reduced inside each CV fold *per block*: 3 video + 2 audio + 2 text = k 7. This stops the highest-variance block dominating (v2.1 S6).
- **M1 (TRIBE):** the 7 TRIBE network features.
- **M2:** M0 + M1.

**Unit and exchangeability** (v2.1 S2, S8)
- **Confirmatory analysis set: one video per company.**
  - For a company with several videos, pick one by a seeded, label-free rule: `random.Random(sha256(company_id + "yct-v2.1")).choice(sorted(video_ids))`.
  - Swapping labels within a pair is invalid, because the accepted video is always the later one; label is confounded with order, era and device.
  - Within-company pairs are EXPLORATORY only.
- **Era strata** come from the YC batch calendar, not from the data: E1 ≤ W2016, E2 S2016–W2020, E3 ≥ S2020.
  - Merge rule: any stratum with fewer than 6 videos merges with its neighbour toward E2.
  - **Era blocks (v2.6 P1; D-047, Referee P1 D6):** E1 + E2 are merged into **E12** ("pre-S2020"), fixed now and label-free (in_primary E1 = 3, E2 = 7). E12/E3 are the levels of the Q(M0) era dummies and the era half of the exchangeability blocks. The manifest keeps the raw `era_stratum` and adds `era_block` (config `era_blocks`). Effective N per block is reported.
  - The Curator applies the merge rule mechanically. The analyst never sees stratum × label counts.
- **Company key (v2.7 FR; D-053, Referee P1 B4).** The manifest carries `company_key` = sha256(salt + company_id)[:12] (salt = config `manifest.company_key.salt`, a non-secret project constant: pseudonymisation against casual joins, not secrecy). Analysis code (cluster bootstrap, StratifiedGroupKFold groups, one-per-company pick from P5 on) uses `company_key` via `manifest.analysis_view`; `company_id` is a curation column only (tested: no `src/yct` analysis module reads it).
- **Exchangeability blocks = era × discovery route** (v2.5 C3; D-040).
  - Blocks are built from `era_block` (E12/E3) × r (v2.6 P1). The opaque codes are produced owner-side by `scripts/owner_label_qc.sh` (era blocks < 6 videos merged within r; codes B1..Bk numbered by a secret-salted hash) and stored in SSM `/yct/owner/blocks`; they are never committed next to the manifest.
  - The Curator records each video's route r ∈ {OPEN, TARGETED} (OPEN = appears in the logged keyword/community search results, whatever found it first; TARGETED = found only by the directory sweep or a rejection hunt). r is stored **only in `data/sealed/`**, never in the manifest; the analyst receives only an opaque, seeded-hash block code.
  - Blocks are era × r, merged by the Curator on **total** counts: a cell with fewer than 6 videos merges with its neighbour era **within the same r**, never across r.
  - Every permutation uses these blocks (H1 labels, H4 rows of R, H4b swaps, per-parcel p, Freedman–Lane); bootstraps are stratified by group × r.
  - r is **never** a model feature (not in M0 or Q): it would put a label proxy into AUC(M0).
  - At unblinding, report the effective N (videos in label-mixed blocks), the label × block table and block-stratified effect sizes next to the pooled ones; effective N < 20 per group → "inconclusive".
  - **Same-route sensitivity**: OPEN only, same W, M0, Q rule, seeds and blocks; outside Holm; T3 survive/fragile rule.

- Every p-value is computed as (b+1)/(B+1).

**Effect sizes**
- Hedges g with a company-cluster BCa bootstrap CI (10,000 resamples).
- ΔAUC with a Nadeau–Bengio-corrected CI. This interval is descriptive only.
- Also report AUC, balanced accuracy and Brier score. Never report PPV or any implied real-world hit rate [B10].

**Two estimands** [B5]
- Unadjusted, and adjusted. The adjusted estimate uses Freedman–Lane under the same blocks. The nuisance set is the first min(5, ⌊N/15⌋) PCs of M0.
- Global signal is NOT a covariate in the primary analysis, because it is a probable mediator.

**SESOI and nulls** [B9]
- SESOI: |d| = 0.5 per network; ΔAUC = 0.05.
- Run TOST at ±0.5 for H1/H2. A result that is non-significant and not equivalent is labelled **inconclusive**. It may never be described as "no difference".

**Sensitivity track T3: photometric normalisation** (v2.3, owner request; D-032; rules v2.4 G-D032)
- Every T1 clip is also run through TRIBE as a photometrically normalised copy:
  - gray-world white balance;
  - one global gain/offset per video, setting mean luma (BT.709 Y′ of gamma-encoded RGB; "luminance" means gamma-encoded luma Y′) and RMS contrast to the config targets. The statistics are estimated on the **V-JEPA2 view** (the source region that becomes the central 256² crop after the 292 short-edge resize); the one global transform is applied to the whole frame, and after-statistics are reported in the crop and over the full frame (v2.4 G-D032);
  - the contrast gain is capped at `max_gain` = 3.0 (floor `min_gain` = 0.25); when the cap binds the offset is re-solved so the mean target still holds, and `gain_capped` is recorded (v2.4 G-D032);
  - within-video dynamics preserved;
  - clipped samples reported (in the crop and over the full frame). Clipping- or cap-flagged clips are **kept** in T3 and reported; a secondary T3 analysis excludes them (v2.4 G-D032).
- T1–T3 differences include one extra lossy encode (v2.4 G-D032).
- H4 and H1 are repeated on T3 as a **pre-specified sensitivity analysis**. T3 never replaces the primary analysis, because altering the stimulus changes what TRIBE sees.
- **T3 analysis rules** (v2.4 G-D032):
  - T3 reuses, unchanged from T1: M0 (computed on T1, never recomputed on T3), Q(M0) (same columns and merges; v2.5 C3), W, the exclusions, the C grid, the era × route exchangeability blocks and the permutation seeds. Only M1 is recomputed from the T3 predictions. The T1 and T3 nulls are therefore paired.
  - The T3 tests sit **outside** the Holm family and spend no α. Report the estimate, CI and uncorrected p, labelled "sensitivity".
  - A T1 effect **survives** if the T3 estimate has the same sign and T3 p < .05 with the same sidedness and the same R construction (H4: ΔAUC_T3 > 0, one-sided permutation p < .05; H1: judged per network, only for networks significant in T1, with the **maxT-adjusted** T3 p over the same 7 networks, same B = 10,000 and the same permutation seeds/blocks as T1 — never the raw per-network p; v2.7 FR, D-053, G-D032 c3 Referee N3). Otherwise it is reported as **"photometrically fragile"**; the T1 verdict is unchanged.
  - T3 can never turn a T1 null into a claim; a T3-only effect is exploratory.
  - The distributions of gain a, channel gains (share with any gain > 1.3), clip fraction and colour cast are reported pooled before unblinding and **by group only after unblinding** (`t3_report`).

**Activation localisation outputs** (v2.3, owner request; D-032; descriptive, not inferential; firewall v2.4 G-D032)
- Input maps: **T1 only**, spatially standardised per TR (union medial wall) and averaged over W, i.e. the H1 scale. Group means of these maps; the difference map exists only after unblinding.
- For each group mean and for the difference map, produce:
  - (a) a parcel table of the top 20 positive and top 20 negative Schaefer-400 parcels **overall** (not per hemisphere), each with its Yeo-7 network, the **best-overlap HCP-MMP area** (the HCP label covering most of the parcel's vertices) with its **overlap fraction** (and the second-best area when the fraction is < 0.5), the modal Destrieux anatomical label, the value, a company-cluster bootstrap CI (resampling unit = company, stratified by group; BCa with a company jackknife; B = 10,000) and a **stability** column (share of bootstrap replicates in which the parcel is in the top 20 of the same sign). The difference table also shows the per-parcel permutation p and its **BH-FDR q** over all 400 parcels (the exploratory parcel family below). Parcels with more than half of their vertices unreliable are not ranked (count reported); the unreliable fraction is shown. **Unreliable vertices (v2.7 FR; D-053, G-D032 c3 Neuro G2)** = the fixed, label-free config `localize.reliability_mask`: the bilateral HCP-MMP1 orbitofrontal (OFC, pOFC, 11l, 13l) and temporal-pole (TGd, TGv) areas minus the union medial wall, 732 vertices, sha256 pinned — regions of susceptibility signal dropout in the gradient-echo BOLD TRIBE was trained on (no per-vertex TRIBE reliability map exists, tribe_requirements §4). ## 2b. WATCHDOG AND INTEGRITY RULES (from reviews/prompt_v1/watchdog.md §2b; binding)

**2b.3 Cadence.** The Watchdog runs at three points:
- after every gate packet, before the votes are counted;

5. **Council.** Every required review exists, has a subagent id, cites evidence, and passes the overlap check.
6. **Cost.** Every Lambda instance is in the ledger, its heartbeat is under 10 minutes, and it is within its lifetime and the budget. Terminate orphans now.
7. **Secrets.** gitleaks is clean on the full history, and the logs contain no key-like strings.
8. **Data/legal.** `DATA_DECISION.md` is signed before any download. Retention is honoured. No founder names or faces appear in outputs. The NC licence is respected.
9. **Loops.** Nothing is past its stuck bound without an escalation file.
10. **Fabrication.** Pick 3 random factual claims (a TRIBE spec, a URL, a citation) and verify each against its source.

**2b.5 Outputs**

**2b.7 Fallibility**

  - Null FPR for H4 is estimated on **synthetic outcomes y\* ~ Bernoulli(logit(g(M0)))**, where g is a pre-specified nonlinear function of the real M0 (squares, products and one cubic term, scaled so AUC(M0) ≈ 0.70) and M1 is real. Use 500 replicates with B = 500, 5 repeats and the full nesting. The acceptable FPR range is 3.1–7.1% (the binomial 95% range for 500 replicates). H1–H3/H4b FPR uses 1,000 label shuffles as before (acceptable range 3.7–6.4%).
