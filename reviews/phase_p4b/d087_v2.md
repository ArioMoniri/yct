## 2. The replacement rule (written before any v2 YuNet run)

Code: `src/yct/tribe_run/locscreen.py` (`select`, `rendered_loc_patches`, `make_gate`/`gate_from_cfg`,
`patch_sha`). Tests: `tests/test_d087.py` and `tests/test_d087_v2.py`.

1. **Candidates:** each category's passes in v4 log order. Rank is the 1-based row in
   `data/refstim/image_<cat>_auto_log_v4.jsonl`; no screen result ever re-orders it.
2. **Start:** slot k of each category holds the k-th pass, i.e. the v1 selection.
3. **Round 0, pre-exclusion** (owner, `pre_excluded`):
   - place_14 (`File:Naantali, Kultaranta 1 kerros, presidentin työhuone 1974 (HK10000-5797).tif`) is
     replaced in its slot by the next unused place pass, before any screen.
   - It is disclosed as post_hoc and cites `outputs/runs/refclips_v1_d085_build2/STOP_NOTE.md`.
   - Why: the build-2 hit was on the x264-decoded patch, which the screen never sees, so the screen alone
     might keep place_14 and fail the same TR again.
4. **Each round:**
   - the 60 slot images go through `locimg.patch`, then joint `locimg.shine(controls.refclips.shine)` over
     all 60 in `loc_categories` order;
   - then the rendered patches the builder will draw (`rendered_loc_patches`, pixel-equal to
     `refclips58.loc_frames`, tested): per LOC clip and event,
     - the intact patch `_u8(_norm(img))`;
     - its scramble (`scramble_seed`, shown outside the events in LOC and LOC_SCR);
     - its swap (`swap_seed`, shown at the LOC_SCR events);
   - then the frozen T-LIC YuNet (`refclips_auto.face_detector`, sha-pinned, score ≥ 0.5, as T-LIC resizes,
     long side 640) on each of the 180 entries.
5. **Gate** (`gate_from_cfg`):
   - the intact patch of a body or place slot (owner Q1);
   - the scramble or swap of every slot, face included (owner "Screen scrambles too").

   Intact face detections are recorded as descriptive and never cause a replacement. `face_absent` lists
   the face slots whose intact rendered patch has no detection; it is descriptive (biostat) and is the
   screen-level forecast of the pooled D-085 face_hit_frac.
6. **Replacement:**
   - Every flagged slot, taken in category order then slot order, gets the next unused pass of its category
     by rank. That includes a face slot flagged on its scramble or swap.
   - A flagged image never returns. A round with no flag ends the loop.
7. **Round cap:** at most 5 rounds. Any slot still flagged after round 5 is a final STOP to the owner, with
   no v3.
8. **Running out of passes:** handled by the continuation in §3, after which the loop re-runs from round 1.
   It is deterministic: the same inputs give the same rounds plus the extension (tested).
9. **Output:** `patch_sha256` holds one sha per slot of the final-round post-SHINE patch. The builder checks
   it (§6).
10. **Guard:** the rule runs on rendered patches only, never on a built clip frame, and refuses until
    `controls.refclips_d087` is frozen.

## 3. The v4 filter continuation (the main path, not an edge case)

Every v4 log ends exactly at its 20th pass (face rank 492, body 533, place 144). So any flag, and the
place_14 pre-exclusion itself, needs the continuation. Place needs at least one more pass before round 1.

- **Driver:** `scripts/locscreen_v2.py`. On `NeedMorePasses(cat, n)` it runs
  `scripts/refstim_auto_run.py run <model_dir> <scratch> --target <cat>=<n>`:
  - the same v4 log and frozen order;
  - every frozen check (the D-079/D-080/D-081 launch preconditions, the audit, the append-only log);
  - only the stop count changes (`effective_need`).
- **Where and cost:**
  - It runs on the yct pod CPU (D-059), with Commons metadata and image reads through the frozen curation
    client and its owner-approved User-Agent. No GPU is used.
  - Expected wall time is about 20 minutes per extra pass (the v4 pass rates were face 20/492, body 20/533,
    place 20/144).
  - The cost is pod time only. It is not a ledger GPU spend, but it is disclosed with the v2 cost.
- **Byte identity:** the driver requires the log to keep every existing byte, so the existing rows stay
  byte-identical (tested). The frozen v1 manifests and `image_manifest_sha256` are never edited: v1 is
  regenerated by no step.
- **Commit before re-screening** (`continuation.committed_before_rescreen`):
  1. After a continuation the driver writes `data/refstim/loc_screen_v2_continuation.json` (target, pass
     count, old and new sha, prefix_identical) and exits 75.
  2. The appended log and that record are committed.
  3. Only then does a re-run start, and it refuses while the logs or the record differ from HEAD (tested).
- **Final STOPs** (`cap_or_exhaustion: stop_owner_final`), each to the owner with no v3:
  - more than 20 extra passes in a category (`max_extra_per_category`);
  - the snapshot exhausted before the needed pass.

## 7. Disclosure (v1 report and v2 report)

- **(h) consumed:** v2 consumes D-071 (h), decided after the build-2 result (`post_hoc: true`). The
  pre-exclusion of place_14 is also post_hoc.
- **Reporting:** every round, flag, score, kind (intact, scramble or swap), replacement, descriptive face
  detection and `face_absent` slot is reported.
- **v1 vs v2:** joint SHINE changes all 60 patches, so the v2 stimuli differ from v1 in every LOC/LOC_SCR
  clip, not only in the replaced slots. v1 stays in the report in full.
- **Partly known in advance:** build-2 QC already showed one v1 outcome, YuNet on the decoded frames: only
  place_14 among the intact body and place images, and no scramble hit. The screen runs on rendered patches,
  not decoded frames, so its round 1 is not identical to that, but it is not blind to it either.
- **No x264 margin:** the screen threshold equals the D-085 QC threshold (0.5), and the screen sees rendered
  patches, not x264-decoded ones. A v2 image can therefore pass the screen and still fail the decoded-frame
  QC, which is a final STOP. The threshold is not lowered; changing it now would be a choice made after a
  result.
- **Screened patch vs the draft:** the screened intact patch is the one the builder draws,
  `_u8(_norm(post-SHINE))`, not the raw post-SHINE patch the draft named. SHINE already sets mean 128 and
  RMS 32, so the two differ only by rounding.
