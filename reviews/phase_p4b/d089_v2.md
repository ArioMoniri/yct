# D-089 v2: SPK speech rate 120 wpm by a stated rule (amends the frozen D-085 part-2 speech key)

Status: v2 for the council re-sign-off (cycle 1: `council_d089_*.md`, REVISE ×5). Nothing is frozen, nothing is
synthesised, no DECISIONS entry yet. Supersedes `d089_draft.md` (kept as history).

## 0. Trigger, owner decisions and history

- **Trigger:** the D-085 part-2 step-2 selection STOPped on queue exhaustion (pod CPU, run at 825c975;
  `outputs/runs/d085p2_selection/speech_selection_v1_STOP.json`, sha256 `25168eca…d995d7`).
  - SPK kept 31 of 60 after all 410 items.
  - All 379 rejects were too short; the median span was 0.79 s at 175 wpm.
  - BURST and TALKFACE were never reached.
- **Owner decision 1 (2026-11-23):** "Slower rate for SPK", offered as 140 wpm with a "median ~1.0–1.1 s".
- **Correction:** the draft's own linear projection gave a 140 wpm median of **0.985 s**, below the 1.0 s floor and
  below what the owner was offered. The pass rate would be 29–45% across slowdown models. All five council members
  asked for the choice to go back to the owner with accurate numbers.
- **Owner decision 2 (2026-11-23, in-session AskUserQuestion, after the correction):**
  - **"120 wpm by rule"**, and
  - **"Final stop, report it"**: an exhaustion in any family is a final STOP. The speech control is reported as
    failed, and every analysis that needs it is dropped and named in the report.

## 1. The SPK rate rule (stated before any D-089 synthesis)

`controls.refclips_d089.spk_rate_rule`: the rate is computed only from the committed v1 STOP spans, with
`source_sha256` pinned.

- **Rule:** take the slowest multiple of 5 wpm below 175 whose **linear** projection (`span × 175 / w`) puts at most
  5% of the 410 v1 SPK spans above 1.5 s.
- **Result:** `speech_corpus.spk_rate_by_rule` gives **120 wpm**. 120 wpm puts 4.6% above 1.5 s; 115 wpm would put
  8.5% above.
- **Tests:**
  - `tests/test_d089_v2.py::test_rule_recomputed_from_the_stop_record_gives_120` recomputes it from the file.
  - `test_rule_boundary_115_is_over_and_120_is_within` checks the boundary.
  - The schema pins SPK = 120, so any other value is refused.

**Projection at 120 wpm** (numbers only, from the STOP record; espeak's slowdown may be sub-linear):

| Slowdown model | Factor | In [1.0, 1.5] s | Too short | Too long | Queue items for 60 SPK keeps |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| linear (e = 1) | 1.458 | 298 / 410 (72.7%) | 93 | 19 | ~83 |
| e = 0.8 | 1.352 | 258 (62.9%) | 147 | 5 | ~95 |
| e = 0.6 | 1.254 | 186 (45.4%) | 221 | 3 | ~132 |

The linear median is 1.149 s, centred in the range. For comparison, 140 wpm gave a median of 0.985 s and
184 / 410 (44.9%) linear. The model is `speech_corpus.spk_sensitivity`, and its values are tested.

- **Joint queue budget:** SPK at 120 wpm uses about 83–132 of the 410 items. That leaves about 278–327 items for
  BURST (15 slots, 2 sentences per attempt) and TALKFACE (3 slots).
  - BURST and TALKFACE have never been measured at 175 wpm, so their exhaustion risk is unknown.
  - This replaces the draft's "at least ~15%" SPK figure.

## 3. Final STOP in every family (stated before the re-run)

A queue exhaustion while filling SPK, BURST or TALKFACE is a **final STOP**. When that happens:

- No further amendment.
- The speech positive control is reported as **failed**, not absent.
- Every analysis that depends on it is **dropped and named** in the v1 report: the SPK/BURST speech-family kernels,
  the AUD-related speech readouts, and the TALKFACE descriptive.
- The STOP record is numbers and ids only. For every family it holds tried, kept, rate and Wilson 95%, plus
  `queue_used_by_family`, `stopped_at` {family, slot, queue_position} and `final_stop: true`.

## 4. Build guards (the build condition is now code, tested before the build ticket)

- `EspeakSynth.args` refuses `rate_wpm=None` when the spec is per-family, so a forgotten argument can never fall back
  to 175. The frozen pre-D-089 path is unchanged.
- `speech_corpus.build_rates` takes the rates from the frozen selection record and asserts:
  - the record's rates equal `effective_spec`;
  - every kept item carries its family's `rate_wpm`.

  Otherwise it STOPs.
- `speech_corpus.build_raw_speech` is the build's RawSpeech binding. It always calls `synth(text, rate_wpm=…)` with
  the record's family rate and refuses an unverified synth. A test confirms SPK is sent at 120, never 175.
- `speech_corpus.clip_speech_meta`: the clip manifest records `rate_wpm` for each clip.
- `refclips_qc85.spk_span_qc` (gating) re-measures each built SPK utterance (the provider output before placement) at
  −40 dBFS. Each must be in [1.0, 1.5] s and equal its selection span within ±1 sample at 48 kHz, otherwise STOP.
- The selection record stores `rate_wpm` for each item and `queue_used_by_family`.
- `speech_corpus.check_no_carry_over` refuses any kept item without a D-089 `rate_wpm`, which is how a copied v1 keep
  would look. A test feeds the 31 v1 keep ids through a fresh selection and shows each was re-synthesised.

## 5. Descriptive rate check (Q1)

`yct.confounds.modspec.peak_modulation_hz` gives the peak of the envelope modulation spectrum (about the syllable
rate) for each speech family, computed on the frozen T-ENV trace.

- It is reported in the v1 report alongside the 120 vs 175 wpm mismatch.
- It never gates and is never used inferentially.
- The T-ENV module itself is unchanged (its frozen blob sha stands).

## 7. Disclosure (v1 report)

- D-089 is a post-hoc amendment made after the v1 selection STOP.
- The rate was first chosen as 140 wpm on a description ("median ~1.0–1.1 s") that its own projection did not
  support (0.985 s). After the correction, the owner chose the rule-based 120 wpm.
- The rule was tuned on the same 410 items that are re-selected. Only spans were used; no outcome data exists for
  stimuli. The sensitivity range (e = 0.6–1.0) is reported.
- SPK (120 wpm) and BURST/TALKFACE (175 wpm) are not rate-matched, a 1.46× difference. The SPK/BURST speech-family
  readouts carry this limitation, and the modulation-peak check of §5 is descriptive.
- The final-STOP ruling covers BURST and TALKFACE, which were never measured.
- The v1 selection record is kept as evidence only; none of its keeps are reused.
