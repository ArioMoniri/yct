**R3. The identical clips are by design, not a build bug.** D-071 (a) says there is one seeded bed (SEED 20261117) and that "visual-only clips carry the control audio bit for bit". The D-085 QC gates also require SPK/BURST audio to equal GREY_CTRL's outside the utterance windows. The builder therefore uses one bed. Its docstring records this as an interpretation.
- GREY_CTRL has no events and no seeded draws, so _00 and _01 are necessarily identical. design_v2 meant them as "one per shift", but both have shift 0 (i // 2 = 0), and a clip without events does not depend on shift anyway.
- FLASH is deterministic: a white frame at the onsets, on the one bed. Clips with the same shift are therefore identical: {0,1,4} have s = 0 and {2,3} have s = −1. This gives exactly the 3 groups that `test_the_frozen_manifest_duplicate_groups_are_known` pins.
- Residual defect, to disclose and not fix: D-071 (b) also lists "the noise bed of each clip" under the per-item seed rule, which contradicts (a). The build follows (a). Rebuilding would change frozen shas.
**R4. Readout: the identical clips are one stimulus, not replicates.** TRIBE inference from one cache entry is deterministic, so the duplicate clips add zero information.
- FLASH has 2 distinct stimuli, and they differ only by a 1 s shift, so the event-locked d_j will be close to identical.
- The K6 clip bootstrap over 5 FLASH clips with 2 GREY-CTRL clips therefore has a CI that is too narrow and weights the groups 3:2.
- No reference readout exists yet, so a fix decided now is still pre-output. Either way, the v1 report must state "FLASH = 2 distinct stimuli, GREY-CTRL = 1".
- No other family is affected: LOC, MOT and SPK have per-item seeds or distinct items, and the test shows no other duplicate groups.
